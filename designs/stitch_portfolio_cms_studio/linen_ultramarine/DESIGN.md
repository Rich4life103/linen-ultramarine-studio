## Brand & Style

This design system serves a dual mandate: an outward-facing portfolio for a high-caliber creative technologist and product designer, paired with an intimate, highly focused content studio and administrative workstation.

The aesthetic philosophy merges **Editorial Craft** with **Technical Precision**. It bridges the tactile, measured calm of Swiss fine-press publishing with the crisp operational clarity required by modern software engineering and design-systems architecture.

- **Brand Personality**: Intellectual, deliberate, tactile, and inventive. Confident without vanity; precise without clinical sterility.
- **Target Audience**: Design leaders, venture partners, engineering collaborators, and cultural institutions seeking systems-level creative direction.
- **Emotional Response**: Grounded calm, aesthetic trust, focused authorial control, and intellectual rigor.
- **Design Style**: Contemporary Editorial paired with Functional Modernism. Generous warm paper canvases, crisp hairline dividers, deep ink-jet typographic contrast, and deliberate bursts of electric ultramarine signal an active digital intellect operating on timeless principles.

## Colors

The palette is anchored in physical bookcraft—warm unbleached paper and carbon ink—interrupted by modern digital signals.

### Surface Architecture
- **Canvas Base (`surface-base`)**: `#FAF9F6` — Warm linen tone serving as the default ground.
- **Canvas Muted (`surface-subtle`)**: `#F4F2EE` — Secondary card backgrounds, table headers, and structural wells.
- **Canvas Sunken (`surface-recessed`)**: `#E8E5DF` — Inset input fields, code blocks, and inactive toggle tracks.
- **Admin Elevated (`surface-elevated`)**: `#FFFFFF` — Sharp white reserved for administrative cards, popovers, and dialogs to establish clean contrast against linen backdrops.

### Ink & Neutral Scale
- **Ink Primary**: `#121316` — High-density carbon black for headlines and critical data.
- **Ink Secondary**: `#2D3139` — Neutral charcoal for running editorial prose and primary form labels.
- **Ink Muted**: `#646A78` — Slate tone for metadata, captions, and secondary actions.
- **Border Hairline**: `#E2E4E9` — Architectural structure and subtle grid dividers.

### Chromatic Signals
- **Electric Ultramarine (`#1D4ED8`)**: The primary kinetic indicator. Used strictly for interactive triggers, focused states, primary buttons, and selected tabs.
- **Terracotta Ochre (`#E06D53` / `#D97706`)**: Secondary expressive accent for critical highlights, award badges, and curated tags.
- **Status Accents**:
  - *Published*: `#059669` (Emerald)
  - *Draft / Pending*: `#D97706` (Amber)
  - *Unsaved / In Review*: `#1D4ED8` (Ultramarine)

## Typography

The typographic architecture balances humanistic cadence with analytical utility across three distinct voices:

1. **Editorial Headlines (`Newsreader`)**: Evokes the tactile craft of broadsheets and academic monographs. Set with optical sizing and standard proportional figures. Display and headline scales leverage negative tracking to maintain density at large sizes.
2. **Interface & Narrative (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Clean, contemporary grotesque with geometric clarity. Handles deep longform case studies, navigation bars, and dense administrative data tables without visual fatigue.
3. **Analytical Metadata (`JetBrains Mono`)**: Strict, technical counterpoint. Reserved for timestamps, status chips, version tags, project index codes (e.g., `[PRJ_042]`), file sizes, and markdown editor source views. Rendered in uppercase with generous letter spacing for chip contexts.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout relies on an asymmetric 12-column fluid grid framed within a maximum container width of `1440px` for portfolio presentations, and a responsive fixed-fluid split pane for the admin dashboard.

### Layout Modalities
- **Portfolio Layout**: Wide editorial margins (`margin-desktop`) allow generous horizontal whitespace. Case studies utilize an offset 8-column center track for body copy with 4-column side rails for analytical marginalia (metadata, figures, tools).
- **Admin Workspace Layout**: A fixed 260px navigation sidebar anchored to a dynamic content stage. Detail editors employ a 70/30 split pane—70% live WYSIWYG writing/design preview, 30% metadata and taxonomy controls.

### Breakpoints & Adaptive Rhythm
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout; gutter `1.5rem`; margin `1.25rem`. Complex side rails stack vertically beneath editorial content. Sticky action bars snap to viewport bottom.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px)**: 8-column layout; gutter `1.5rem`; margin `2.5rem`.
- **Desktop (> 1024px)**: 12-column layout; gutter `2.5rem`; margin `4rem`.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth in this system avoids heavy theatrical dropshadows, favoring **Tonal Layers** and **Crisp Structural Outlines** punctuated by targeted ambient diffusion.

### Stratification Rules
- **Layer 0 (Canvas Base)**: `#FAF9F6` — The paper ground upon which all content is inked.
- **Layer 1 (Recessed Well)**: `#F4F2EE` — Inset areas, sidebar rails, and inactive zones; bounded by `1px solid #E2E4E9`.
- **Layer 2 (Elevated Card / Surface)**: `#FFFFFF` — Active work cards, content blocks, and tables. Defined by a sharp `1px solid #E2E4E9` and a micro-diffusion ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(18, 19, 22, 0.04), 0 6px 16px rgba(18, 19, 22, 0.02)`.
- **Layer 3 (Floating Overlays / Modals / Toolbars)**: `#FFFFFF` or Frosted Canvas (`rgba(250, 249, 246, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`). Elevated using an editorial drop: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(18, 19, 22, 0.08), 0 2px 6px rgba(18, 19, 22, 0.03)` with a hairline outline (`rgba(18, 19, 22, 0.08)`).

### Focus & Hover Kinetic Elevation
Interactive cards do not translate upward on hover. Instead, their border transitions from `#E2E4E9` to `#121316` (or `#1D4ED8` for active tools), accompanied by an ambient shadow deepening from `0.04` to `0.07` opacity.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a structured, architectural form language (`roundedness: 1` — Soft, with intentional pill-shaped exceptions for atomic status labels).

- **Standard Containers & Cards**: `0.25rem` (4px) or `0.5rem` (8px). Structural cards and content panes preserve sharp, rectilinear corners reminiscent of tailored print spreads.
- **Form Inputs & Button Defaults**: `0.375rem` (6px). Provides ergonomic clickability without becoming cartoonish or bubbly.
- **Pills & Metatags**: `9999px` (Fully rounded). Exclusively applied to atomic status indicators, category chips, and floating navigation pills to create immediate visual contrast against geometric card grids.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Solid `#1D4ED8` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `font-family: Plus Jakarta Sans; font-weight: 600`. Padding: `0.625rem 1.25rem`. Border-radius: `0.375rem`. Hover state transitions to `#1E40AF`.
- **Secondary (Editorial)**: `#FFFFFF` surface, `1px solid #E2E4E9` border, `#121316` text. Hover transitions border to `#121316` with zero background shift.
- **Ghost / Tertiary**: Transparent surface, `#2D3139` text. Hover triggers `background-color: #F4F2EE`.
- **Destructive**: Subdued `#FEF2F2` background with `#DC2626` text, transitioning on active confirmation to solid `#DC2626` with `#FFFFFF` text.

### 2. Status Chips & Pill Tags
- **Taxonomy Pill**: Height `24px`, padding `0 10px`, border-radius `9999px`. Uses `JetBrains Mono` (`code-xs`). Background: `#F4F2EE`, border: `1px solid #E2E4E9`, text: `#2D3139`.
- **Admin Status Badges**:
  - *Published*: Background `rgba(5, 150, 105, 0.08)`, border `1px solid rgba(5, 150, 105, 0.25)`, text `#047857`. Left dot: 6px solid `#059669`.
  - *Draft*: Background `rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.08)`, border `1px solid rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.25)`, text `#B45309`. Left dot: 6px solid `#D97706`.
  - *Unsaved*: Background `rgba(29, 78, 216, 0.08)`, border `1px solid rgba(29, 78, 216, 0.25)`, text `#1D4ED8`. Left dot: 6px solid `#1D4ED8`.

### 3. Project Cards (Portfolio)
- Clean, uncluttered layout framed by `1px solid #E2E4E9`.
- Top aspect ratio: `16:10` media well with subtle inner hairline border.
- Bottom metadata pane: Newsreader title (`headline-sm`) paired with a metadata lockup (`JetBrains Mono` year, taxonomy pill, client name).
- Interaction: Cursor triggers a custom magnetic view pill; border shifts to `#121316` on desktop pointer hover.

### 4. Input Fields & Text Areas
- Background `#FFFFFF` (in admin context) or `#F4F2EE` (in linen public context).
- Border: `1px solid #E2E4E9`.
- Font: `Plus Jakarta Sans`, 14px (`body-sm`).
- Focus Ring: `0 0 0 1px #1D4ED8` paired with border color `#1D4ED8`. No fuzzy glow or colored drop shadow.
- Label: `label-sm` with uppercase treatment and `letter-spacing: 0.05em`.

### 5. Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Checkbox: `16px x 16px`, `0.25rem` radius, `1px solid #646A78`. Checked state fills `#1D4ED8` with a crisp white tick mark.
- Radio: `16px x 16px`, circular. Checked state displays a solid 6px `#1D4ED8` bullseye surrounded by white space inside the border ring.

### 6. Modal Sheets & Drawers
- Admin details employ a sliding right-hand drawer (width: `560px` desktop) anchored to the viewport frame with a 1px vertical border (`#E2E4E9`).
- Modals rest on a softened canvas veil (`rgba(18, 19, 22, 0.4)` with 4px backdrop blur). Modal body features sharp white surfaces with an editorial serif title and a fixed bottom utility bar.

### 7. Floating Action Toolbar
- Bottom-docked floating palette: `height: 48px`, border-radius: `9999px`, background: `rgba(18, 19, 22, 0.95)`, backdrop-filter: `blur(8px)`.
- Icon and text labels render in `#FAF9F6`. Dividers inside the toolbar use `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`.
- Primary actions inside the bar glow subtly with ultramarine badges.
